More, More, More · Two captures, weeks apart

When Is It Enough?

First, we are told to buy it. Then we are asked why we still have it.

Select either image to view it full size.

The closet is already overflowing, and there is still something else to want. Weeks later, another post asks whether people will keep wearing things now that their trends have passed. I agree with using what we already own, but I also notice how quickly buying something becomes another reason to judge someone.

Together, these captures make me wonder when the task is ever finished. We are expected to know what to buy, which version to buy, and when to stop wanting it. Even keeping something we like becomes a choice we have to explain. Why does enjoying what we own need anyone else's approval?

Which of these expectations have you caught yourself treating as your own?
