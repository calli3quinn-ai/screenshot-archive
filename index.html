Annotation 01

Needing The New Stuff

Influencers, brands, and marketing constantly convince us that we need something new, even when our closets are overflowing and our bank accounts are already strained.

The search bar at the top, “samyra old navy,” shows this cycle in action. Someone sees what an influencer bought, searches for the same items, and is encouraged to buy them too.
